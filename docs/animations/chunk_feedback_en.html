Presenter notes and implementation basis

Efficiency improves by assigning less subsequent data to the slow rail—not by repairing a link or migrating in-flight data. A hard disconnect with no completion feedback requires additional recovery mechanisms.

Initially, only two chunks are launched. The first completion on either rail permits up to eight active chunks; the sender does not wait for both probes. A rail without a completed sample temporarily has zero estimated-rate weight.
