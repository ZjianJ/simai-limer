LIMER · CONCEPT DEMONSTRATION

Adapt the next chunk.
Reduce slow-path bottlenecks.

Continuous completion feedback guides the allocation of fixed-size chunks across two rails. When one rail slows down, new feedback shifts subsequent assignments toward the faster rail—without waiting for the entire collective to finish.

Open English animation ↗GitHub repositoryDemo scope & validation

1. Observe completions

Use chunk size divided by first-transmission-to-completion-ACK time. This is a completion-rate estimate, not a physical-bandwidth measurement.

2. Accrue byte credits

Normalize the latest per-rail rates into weights. Add proportional byte credits and assign the next whole chunk to the eligible rail with the larger credit.

3. Adapt continuously

Update on every completion. In-flight chunks stay on their assigned rail; changes affect subsequent assignments and therefore have feedback delay.
